Privacy policy

This app is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own YouTube channels.

Data accessed: YouTube upload and channel-management access for the owner's channels, granted through Google sign-in.

Use: the access is used only to upload videos, set their metadata and schedule, and read basic channel statistics. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage: sign-in tokens are stored privately by the owner and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
