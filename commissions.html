File 05 / How it works

Paying

  1. Tell me what you need. A DM is enough: what it should do and roughly when you need it.
  2. I quote it. You get a price and a scope before any money moves. A DM counts as agreeing.
  3. You pay. When depends on the size of the job (below).
  4. I build it and show you. You test it as it comes together. Small tweaks are included; bigger changes get their own quote.
  5. Done. It's handed over working.

Good to know

  • The timeline is agreed before I start. I won't promise a date I can't keep.
  • Prices on this page are starting prices; the quote is what counts.
  • Security work is the basics. I add sensible protection and tell you what it doesn't cover. It isn't an audit, a penetration test or a guarantee, and I won't say it is.
  • Not on the menu: Minecraft builds. Anything else, ask. If I can't do it well I'll say no, not take your money and wing it.
