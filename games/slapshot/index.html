file 04 / how it plays

The right stick is your hockey stick.

  • Hold back, flick forward. Pull the right stick back to wind up and flick it forward to shoot. The puck goes the way you flick, and a dashed line shows the aim while you hold.
  • Wrist or slap. A wind-up under 0.4 s is a wrist shot that the goalie reads late. Keep holding for a slapshot: harder up to 1 s, but you slow down and the goalie sees it coming.
  • One-timers. Wind up before a pass reaches you and flick as it lands. Pass across the crease and shoot before the goalie slides over.
  • Hands and hits. Keep the blade moving and poke checks miss more. Land a check and they drop the puck. Miss a check or a poke and you stumble for a moment.
