# Slapshot (hockey)

Top-down 3-on-3 arcade hockey. **These controls are a proposal** (Jay gave no specifics).

- **Skate:** left stick; skating has momentum, L2 digs in for a hard stop.
- **The right stick is the hockey stick.** Sweep it side to side to stickhandle (the puck follows the blade). Pull it back and flick forward to shoot: a short flick is a wrist shot, a long wind-up is a slapshot, harder but easier to read and block.
- **Pass:** R1 to the teammate you're facing, R2 for a saucer pass over sticks.
- **Defense:** L1 body check, right stick poke check toward the puck. Switch player with Cross / A.
- **No stat training.** Short periods, the goalie is computer-controlled.
- Keyboard: WASD skate, mouse drag or arrows for the stick, Shift stop, Space switch.
