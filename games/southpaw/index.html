file 05 / how it plays

Your triggers are your hands.

  • Pull sets the power. A light pull is a quick shot. A full pull lands heavy, costs more stamina and leaves you open longer if it misses.
  • The stick picks the punch. Centre throws a straight, sideways a hook, down an uppercut. Hold the left stick down to go to the body. Hooks and uppercuts need you close.
  • Guard, slip, counter. High guard stops head shots, low guard the body. Slip a straight or hook and your next punch is a counter that lands harder.
  • Three rounds, ten point must. Every punch costs stamina and tired arms are slow. You go down when your health runs out. Three knockdowns and the referee stops it.
