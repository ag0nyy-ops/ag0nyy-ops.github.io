file 03 / how it plays

Aim it with both hands.

  • No meter. Hold shoot to rise. The view drops into your eyes and the world slows down. Let go whenever both hands sit in the ring on the rim.
  • Two sticks, two hands. The hollow circle is your guide hand (left stick), the filled one your shooting hand (right stick). The closer to the middle, the better.
  • Defense shrinks the ring. A hand in your face or a defender in the air makes it smaller. Wait them out or get it off first.
  • Street rules. Make it, lose it. After a steal or a defensive board, take it back past the arc. Twos inside, threes outside.
