# Bucket (basketball)

Half-court arcade basketball, local first (1v1, then 2v2 and 3v3). Online 5v5 is a later idea.

- **Moving:** left stick moves, right stick dribble moves (crossover, behind the back) by direction, R1 pass, L1 call for screen.
- **Shooting has no timing meter and no green line.** Hold Square / X (or L2+R2) to rise: the view drops into first person at the rim. Each stick is one hand: the left stick aims the guide hand, the right stick the shooting hand. Both have to sit on the rim's target for it to drop; how far off each one is decides make or miss, and release is just letting go.
- **Defense:** stay in front, contest by raising a hand (stick up), which tightens the shooter's target.
- **No stat training.**
- Keyboard: WASD move, mouse or IJKL + arrows for the two hands in the shot view.
