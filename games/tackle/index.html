file 02 / how it plays

One hand has the ball. Only that hand plays.

  • Random hand. Every play the ball goes in your left or right hand. Only that side's stick and trigger do anything, so muscle memory can't run the play.
  • Throw it. Hold the trigger to drop back and aim with the stick. Hold longer and pull deeper for more zip. Let go to throw.
  • Run it. The stick runs. Flick it sideways to juke right as a tackler arrives. Hold the trigger to burst or stiff-arm while the meter lasts.
  • Short drives. You play offense and their drives are simulated in between. On fourth down you can punt or kick. Players never get stats, you get better.
