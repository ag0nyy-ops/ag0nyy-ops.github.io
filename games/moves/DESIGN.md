# Moves (gymnastics)

Side-on floor routine judged on what your body actually does. **These controls are a proposal** (Jay gave no specifics).

- **Left stick drives the arms, right stick the legs.** Run-up, hurdle and takeoff come from setting both at the right moments.
- **In the air:** L2 / R2 tuck and pike (how far you pull = how tight, which spins faster). Let go to open up and slow the rotation for the landing. Flips and twists come from the right stick direction at takeoff.
- **Landing:** feet under you, arms up, still. Judges score difficulty (what you threw) and execution (deductions for bent knees, open tuck, steps, falls), shown move by move after the routine.
- **Routine:** string passes together across the floor; you choose what to throw, nothing is unlocked by grinding.
- **No flashing:** the score reveal and crowd stay calm.
- Floor first, then beam or vault later.
- Keyboard: WASD arms, arrows legs, F / J tuck and pike.
